# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📊 End-to-End Data Analytics Project | Python • MySQL • Power BI

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding customer shopping behavior, purchasing patterns, product performance, discounts, subscriptions, shipping preferences, and customer segments.

The project demonstrates a complete analytics workflow — from raw data cleaning and transformation in Python to business analysis using MySQL and interactive visualization in Power BI.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Understanding customer purchasing behavior is important for improving product strategy, customer retention, promotional campaigns, and revenue performance.

In this project, customer shopping data was analyzed to answer key business questions such as:

- How does revenue differ between male and female customers?
- Do customers who use discounts make higher-value purchases?
- Which products receive the highest average ratings?
- How does average purchase value differ between shipping methods?
- Do subscribers generate higher revenue or average spending?
- Which products have the highest proportion of discounted purchases?
- How can customers be segmented based on previous purchases?
- What are the top-selling products within each category?
- Are repeat buyers more likely to subscribe?
- Which age groups contribute the most revenue?

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this analysis were to:

- Understand customer purchasing patterns
- Analyze revenue contribution across customer groups
- Identify high-performing products
- Evaluate the impact of discounts
- Compare subscriber and non-subscriber behavior
- Analyze customer purchasing frequency
- Identify repeat and loyal customer segments
- Understand revenue contribution by age group
- Provide data-driven insights for business decision-making
